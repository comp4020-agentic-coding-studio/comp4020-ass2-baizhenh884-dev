# Disagreement is information

Back of the Envelope · Week 6

---

## Most people, in most rooms, reach for five hundred

Averaging is a reasonable response to exactly one situation: two attempts to
measure the **same quantity**, each carrying error that could fall either
way.

That situation is rarer than the reflex suggests.

disagreements resolve into "these were never the same quantity."

## A moment against a period

A count of who is in a condition on one **night** is a different kind of
number from a count of everyone who passed through that condition across a
**year**.

They can land close, far apart, or in either order — matching the label is
not the same as matching the grain.

## Near-agreement reads as corroboration

Occasionally close numbers really do confirm each other. More often it is
coincidence between quantities that were never comparable — and this
version of the error survives longest, because it never produces an
argument.
