# One miss tells you nothing. Fifteen might.

Back of the Envelope · Week 12

---

## 70%, and right

Compatible with excellent judgment. Compatible with terrible judgment.
Compatible with chance.

One result cannot tell the three apart.

---

## A pattern is not "I was often right"

Being right does not need explaining. A pattern is something like:

- always missing on the same side
- most overconfident about quantities read somewhere, not built
- widening every range that involved someone else's number

Name the actual shape — not a summary of how the semester felt.

---

## Now go looking for the entry that ruins it

Confirming a pattern and searching for its exceptions use the record in
opposite directions. Doing only the first produces a claim that only ever
meets evidence that agrees with it.

Then swap diaries. Your partner reads for what would undermine your claim
— not for whether it sounds right.
