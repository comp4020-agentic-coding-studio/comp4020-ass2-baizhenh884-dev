# Policies and support

## Late work and extensions

If you expect to miss a due date, contact the convenor before it passes.

## Academic integrity

An estimate is only as good as its disclosed assumptions. In this course,
using a published figure, a proxy dataset or a borrowed method in assessed
work without saying so is the integrity failure that matters — not the
number you land on.

## Use of AI tools

You may use AI tools while working through the course. You remain
responsible for verifying every claim, source and assumption they produce —
an AI's confidence is not evidence. Disclose meaningful AI assistance in your
assessed reasoning: what you asked for, and what you kept.

## Getting help

Ask during your tutorial first. Email your tutor when a question can't wait
until the next one; email the convenor for anything course-level rather than
specific to your own work.
