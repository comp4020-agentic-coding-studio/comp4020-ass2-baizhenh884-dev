## What sits inside each one

Two assessments, but neither is a single sitting. Each is built from parts
handed in at different points, and each part carries its own share of that
assessment's mark — the shares below are of the assessment, not of the course.

<AssessmentStructure />

## How assessment develops through the semester

Six weeks put something into one of the two pieces above. None of them is a
third assessment, and none carries a mark of its own: what the room produces
is carried into the Diary or the estimate and marked there. Week 11 is the one
exception, and it is not an extra — it is the hearing stage of the Client
Estimate, happening live.

<AssessmentMilestones />

Weeks 5 to 7 sit outside this list on purpose. They train the method the
written estimate is built from — the bridge from a borrowed number to the one
you want, what to do when two sources disagree, and holding a figure under
pressure — but nothing from those rooms is handed in.
